# M10e Systems

**Maintainable engineering for teams who prefer clarity over chaos.**

M10e Systems is the independent consulting practice of Kevin Barrett.
I work with small engineering teams who are smart, resource-constrained, and slightly weary of systems that behave like temperamental housecats: fine one minute, inscrutable the next.

If you need durable automation, documentation with a pulse, or someone who can enter a mildly disordered system and leave it tidier than he found it — welcome.

---

## What I Actually Do

### **DevOps & Automation**
Pipelines, deployments, reproducible environments — the practical machinery that keeps a team moving.
I’m allergic to Rube Goldberg architectures and YAML Jenga towers.
The solutions I build tend to be simple, explicit, and resistant to future shenanigans.

### **Documentation That Isn’t an Afterthought**
Documentation is the collective memory of a team.
When it fails, everything else degrades.
I write docs that someone can pick up six months later without muttering imprecations at their predecessor.

### **Technical Mentorship**
Sometimes the real problem is conceptual fog.
I help teams untangle their architecture, articulate what they’re actually trying to build, and develop workflow habits that reduce entropy rather than amplify it.

No guruism — just practical guidance and a bit of engineering anthropology.

---

## How I Work

I show up, learn your system, and make it make sense.

If a deployment process requires a secret incantation, we de-ritualize it.
If your infrastructure depends on Eric remembering to “just restart that one pod after deploy,” we fix the root cause.
If everything feels fragile, we stabilize first and optimize later.

My north star is maintainability.
Elegant is good. Understandable is better. Durable is best.
