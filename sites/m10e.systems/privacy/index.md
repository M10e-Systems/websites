# Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 20, 2026

M10e Systems uses this website and related social channels to share consulting information and receive business inquiries.

## Information collected

If you contact M10e Systems by email or through a social platform, the information you choose to send may include your name, email address, organization, message content, and any context needed to respond to your inquiry.

This website may also receive basic technical information from normal web hosting and analytics infrastructure, such as IP address, browser type, referring page, and request timestamps.

## How information is used

Information is used to:

- respond to inquiries
- discuss possible consulting work
- maintain website and account security
- understand whether published material is useful

M10e Systems does not sell personal information.

## Third-party services

This website may link to or interact with third-party services such as LinkedIn, Facebook, Instagram, X, GitHub, email providers, hosting providers, or analytics tools. Those services are governed by their own privacy policies.
